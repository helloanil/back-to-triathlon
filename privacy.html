Back to Triathlon

Privacy

Updated November 23, 2026

Back to Triathlon is a private personal training planner. Its public website contains the application interface; access to saved training and account connections requires the owner's private access key.

Training and account connections

Saved training records and calendar edits are stored in the private Cloudflare backend. Garmin and Google connection credentials are encrypted on the server. They are not included in the public GitHub website or returned to the browser.

Google Calendar

Connecting Google is optional. Google asks for permission before calendar access begins. You can choose a separate calendar created by this app, or grant access to an existing calendar you can edit.

The connection publishes planned workout titles, instructions, dates, start times and durations. It reads changes to the app's linked workout events so moved or resized sessions can update the training planner. Google edits do not replace recorded training results. Unrelated appointments are not imported into the planner. Personal notes below the event's “Personal calendar notes” label are retained when workout instructions change.

The backend stores the selected calendar ID, encrypted authorization tokens, event identifiers, synchronization checkpoints and the linked workout details needed to compare changes. This Google data is used to provide calendar synchronization. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial intelligence models.

Hosting and sharing

GitHub Pages hosts the public interface. Cloudflare hosts the private service and database. Connecting Google sends the planned event details to Google Calendar. The app does not invite attendees, send calendar invitation emails, or make a calendar public. These providers process requests under their respective policies.

Disconnecting and retention

Use Disconnect in the Google Calendar connection page to remove the saved Google authorization tokens and stop synchronization. You can also revoke the app's access from your Google Account permissions. Disconnecting keeps existing Google events, training records and event-link information so a deliberate reconnection can continue without creating duplicate workouts. Removing Google events or saved training is a separate action.

Browser storage

The browser stores a private dashboard session for up to 30 days. Sign out to remove that browser's session. Keep the dashboard access key private. The app does not add advertising or analytics trackers.

Questions

For software questions, contact the maintainer through the project repository. Do not post credentials or private training information in public issues.
